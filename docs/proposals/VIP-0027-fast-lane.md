## Summary

A cheap first screen that may settle only the contract's declared safe default, with calibration on a held-out set, and escalates everything else to a full jury. Measured against full juries before it is offered.

## Motivation

Every contract summons a full jury, even when the answer is plainly the safe default.

## Done when

Calibrated on a published set, with the share escalated and the disagreement rate measured.
