## Summary

Snapshot the stake delegated to the vote account at the moment of the claim (from stake accounts, or a stake pool's records) and distribute the bond pro rata through a claim account each staker draws from.

## Motivation

A broken commission covenant pays its bond to one beneficiary account, not to each staker.

## Done when

Stakers of a broken covenant each claim their share on devnet.
