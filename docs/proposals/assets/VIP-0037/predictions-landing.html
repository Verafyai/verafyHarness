Prediction markets that resolve on evidence, not a price feed.

Prediction markets on Ethereum and Solana settle through oracles, and an oracle can only report one number or one yes from one feed. So every market is a single simple question.

A Verafy jury reads named sources, quotes what it found, and rules on structured claims. That means markets with stages, conditions, numeric ranges and rankings, and an honest answer when the evidence is not there: unresolvable, and your stake comes back.

How a market resolves

  1. The jury reads the named sources at resolve time, with web search, through Prompt Guard.
  2. It rules Yes, No, a value, or Unresolvable, quoting the evidence with links and retrieval times.
  3. The ruling posts on Solana and a challenge window opens.
  4. Anyone can challenge with a bond. A larger, fresh panel decides. Then winners claim.

Pools, not a house

Each outcome has a USDC pool. Winners split the losing pools. Nobody takes the other side of your bet but other bettors. No token.

"I don't know" is a ruling

If sources vanish, disagree past the template's rule, or the event is cancelled, the jury says Unresolvable and stakes come back.

Markets we will not open

Nothing on a person's death or harm, on violence, on private individuals, or on outcomes one participant controls.
