## Summary

Each bounty pays from a policy wallet. A jury rules whether a submission met the spec, with the spec and the submission pinned as evidence.

## The problem today

Bounty payouts wait on reviewers, and contested submissions take longest.

## What the jury decides

- Whether the submission meets the bounty's stated spec.

## Why it pays

- Speed: reviewers stop being the bottleneck.
- Customer satisfaction: contributors get a reasoned ruling and an appeal.

These are described, not estimated: the pilot is how they get measured.

## What we measure

- Bounties released
- Appeals and overturns

## Shape

Treasury template with a payee list built per bounty.

## Done when

Twenty bounties paid on rulings.

Then this VAP moves to Live, and to Closed when the result is published.
