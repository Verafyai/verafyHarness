## Summary

Offer Covenant Launch as a launch option. The creator's supply goes into a Squads wallet with a Judiciary Signer and the covenant-launch policy, and the token page shows a covenant badge linking to the policy and the release history.

## The problem today

Launches end with insiders selling into buyers. Launchpads compete on anti-sniper features, but none binds the creator's own supply.

## What the jury decides

- Every release from the covenant wallet.

## Why it pays

- Makes money: a paid launch tier.
- Keeps customers: buyers return to launchpads where they were not dumped on.

These are described, not estimated: the pilot is how they get measured.

## What we measure

- Launches with a covenant
- 30-day buyer retention, covenant against non-covenant launches
- Releases refused, and why

## Shape

Verafy runs the jury and builds the launch option alongside the launchpad team.

## Done when

Fifty covenant launches, with the retention comparison published.

Then this VAP moves to Live, and to Closed when the result is published.
