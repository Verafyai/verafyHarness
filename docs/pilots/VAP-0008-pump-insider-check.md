## Summary

At launch, a jury rules from funding paths and timing which early wallets are tied to the creator. Those wallets are bound by the covenant, or flagged on the token page.

## The problem today

Creators split their own buys across many wallets, and snipers take early supply. Buyers can't tell which wallets are the creator's.

## What the jury decides

- Which early wallets are tied to the creator.

## Why it pays

- Saves money: for buyers.
- Makes money: for the launchpad, as a trust feature the others lack.

These are described, not estimated: the pilot is how they get measured.

## What we measure

- Insider wallets found
- False positives found on appeal

## Shape

Rulings on chain data only. No personal data about wallet owners is collected.

## Done when

One thousand launches checked, with false positives measured on appeal.

Then this VAP moves to Live, and to Closed when the result is published.
