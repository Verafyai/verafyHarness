## Summary

Card photos, grading records, vault photos and the buyer's delivery photo, with a one-time code in the shot, go to a jury. Its ruling releases escrow, triggers the buyback, or refunds.

## The problem today

Physical cards are sold and redeemed on chain, but condition and delivery disputes are settled by support staff.

## What the jury decides

- Whether the card delivered is the card sold, in the stated condition.

## Why it pays

- Speed: rulings in minutes, not days of support.
- Saves money: fewer "never arrived" and condition refunds paid on a guess.

These are described, not estimated: the pilot is how they get measured.

## What we measure

- Disputes resolved and time to resolve
- Agreement with human reviewers on the same cases

## Shape

The asset-transfer design in VIP-0015. Needs multimodal juries.

## Done when

One hundred disputes ruled, with agreement against human reviewers published.

Then this VAP moves to Live, and to Closed when the result is published.
