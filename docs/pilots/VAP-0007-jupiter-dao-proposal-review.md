## Summary

Before a vote, a jury reads the proposal against the DAO's stated rules and publishes what it changes for holders, and whether it breaks a stated rule.

## The problem today

Voters read long forum threads to learn what a proposal changes for them.

## What the jury decides

- Whether the proposal breaks a stated DAO rule, and a summary of what it changes.

## Why it pays

- Speed: voters read a ruling, not a thread.
- Customer satisfaction: fewer surprises after votes.

These are described, not estimated: the pilot is how they get measured.

## What we measure

- Proposals reviewed before votes
- Voter reads of the ruling

## Shape

Advisory first: the ruling informs the vote and moves no funds.

## Done when

Every JUP DAO proposal for one quarter reviewed before its vote.

Then this VAP moves to Live, and to Closed when the result is published.
