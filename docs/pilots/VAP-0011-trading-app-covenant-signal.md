## Summary

Show whether a token's supply is under a covenant, with the insider ruling, and add a filter for it in copy trading and sniping.

## The problem today

Trading apps show price and volume, not whether supply can be dumped.

## What the jury decides

- None per trade: the signal reads covenant state and launch rulings.

## Why it pays

- Keeps customers: fewer users lose money on rugs.
- Makes money: a paid safety filter.

These are described, not estimated: the pilot is how they get measured.

## What we measure

- Users who turn the filter on
- Losses on filtered against unfiltered tokens

## Shape

An API for covenant state and launch rulings.

## Done when

The signal live in one trading app.

Then this VAP moves to Live, and to Closed when the result is published.
