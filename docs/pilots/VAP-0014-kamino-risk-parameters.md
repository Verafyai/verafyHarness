## Summary

Parameter updates go through a Judiciary Signer that checks them against the published risk framework.

## The problem today

Parameter updates by a risk team move user risk with a few signatures.

## What the jury decides

- Whether an update is within the published risk framework.

## Why it pays

- Saves money: bad parameter pushes are caught before they execute.

These are described, not estimated: the pilot is how they get measured.

## What we measure

- Changes reviewed
- Changes refused

## Shape

The upgrade-authority template, adapted to parameter changes.

## Done when

One quarter of parameter changes executed through the signer.

Then this VAP moves to Live, and to Closed when the result is published.
