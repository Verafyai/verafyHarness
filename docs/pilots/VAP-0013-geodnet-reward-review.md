## Summary

Flagged stations go to a jury with the station's data and its neighbors' data. It rules before rewards are clawed back.

## The problem today

Stations that spoof positions earn rewards. Heuristic clawbacks also hit honest stations.

## What the jury decides

- Whether a flagged station's data shows spoofing.

## Why it pays

- Saves money: rewards paid to fraud.
- Customer satisfaction: honest stations aren't clawed back by a heuristic alone.

These are described, not estimated: the pilot is how they get measured.

## What we measure

- Cases ruled
- Agreement with the Geodnet team on the same cases

## Shape

Evidence comes from their data exports, pinned by hash.

## Done when

Two hundred flagged stations ruled, with agreement published.

Then this VAP moves to Live, and to Closed when the result is published.
