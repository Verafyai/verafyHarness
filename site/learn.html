Learn

Every concept in Verafy Harness and Verafy Chain, one page each, in plain words. Each page says what is built and what is not, with a link to the requirement in the specification.

Basics

  • Actually Smart Contracts: A contract written in words that an AI jury rules on, with pinned evidence and exits that settle exactly.
  • A harness: One AI model wrapped with everything a validator needs: intake, context checks, a signer, a bond, a runtime and a routing agent.
  • Juries, commit and reveal: How a jury of harnesses from different model families rules: drawn from the beacon, commit first, reveal second, agreement against a bar.
  • Open-weight models and model families: Any open-weight model from Hugging Face can sit on a jury. Diversity is counted by model family: the base model a model descends from.
  • Agreement and the bar: A ruling passes only when enough independent jurors agree; below the bar, nothing is decided and the contract escalates.
  • Proof of Judgment: The record that makes a ruling checkable: who sat, what each committed and revealed, and the exit that ran.
  • Context is evidence: Nothing in a contract's context can instruct a juror. Instructions found in evidence are reported as evidence.
  • The economics: What a ruling costs, where the money goes, and why packing and routing matter.

Stake

  • Bonds and slashing: Running a harness means posting a bond that can be taken for misbehavior. What each fault costs, and where the money goes.
  • The jury scheduler: The one place that decides who sits on a jury, when each step is due, and what happens when a juror does not show.
  • The randomness beacon: Jury seeds that no block sealer can steer: a hash chain each sealer fixes in advance.
  • Operators: Model families keep one model's blind spots from deciding. Operators keep one owner from holding several seats.
  • The challenge window and challenge bonds: Payouts wait until a ruling can no longer be overturned cheaply, so an overturn moves money, not just the record.

Settlement

  • Exits and TinySVM: The AI chooses an exit; TinySVM runs it with exact integer arithmetic that every node can re-run.
  • Verafy Chain: A chain whose blocks are rulings: nodes hold funded contracts, draw juries and seal blocks; harnesses do the judging.
  • Settling on Solana: The verafy_ruling program runs commit, reveal and the Ruling account on Solana today, with no validator changes.
  • The light client: How a harness reads other chains: several independent sources that must agree, never one RPC.
  • The encrypted queue: Contracts wait sealed until their jury is drawn, so nobody can front-run a ruling.

Proofs

  • Assurance levels: L0 to L3: how much proof a contract asks for, from a plain jury to proofs of inference.
  • Proofs of inference: A cryptographic proof that a specific model produced a specific answer to a specific prompt.
  • Proof packing: Break contracts into small questions, merge the ones they share, and prove each 1,024-token window once.
  • Atoms and plans: The small questions proof packing works with, and the exact rule that composes them.

Routing

  • Multi-model routing: Start every question at the cheapest tier that can answer it safely, and move up only on disagreement.
  • Fast screens and calibration: A fast model may settle a question only where its accuracy is proven, per kind of question, and the proof is rechecked live.
  • Disclosed reasoning: Where a juror's reasoning is kept, what it costs to store, and what goes on chain.

Money

  • The routing agent: The part of a harness that holds keys and pays bills: keyblock, payment wallet, API router and the Banker.
  • The Banker: A deterministic service, not an AI agent, that turns deposits in any allowed token into the target token, with every step recorded.
  • API credits: How a harness keeps its model accounts funded, and why most top-ups are still manual.
  • Coin-free: Why there is no coin, and how harnesses get paid without one.

Governance

  • Audits and challenges: How a sealed ruling is re-checked, and how it can be overturned.
  • The Constitution and the ethics gate: What Verafy will never rule on, and how every contract is screened before its jury.

Running one

  • The harness daemon: The long-running process that keeps a harness online: register, watch, commit, reveal, prove.
  • Launching a harness: Every way to run one: this machine, Docker, DigitalOcean, Hetzner, any cloud, or a one-click button.
  • A local testnet: A whole testnet on one machine, with scripted AI so it costs nothing, and optional Solana settlement.

Verafy Harness is the validator for Verafy Chain, a Verafy research concept. It is a prototype, not a running network, and there is no token.
