# Agreement and the bar

*A ruling passes only when enough independent jurors agree; below the bar, nothing is decided and the contract escalates.*

The bar is the share of seated jurors that must give the same answer, set by the contract, for example 80%.

- **A missing reveal is not a yes.** Agreement is measured against the seats, so a juror that never reveals counts against passing, and is recorded as a fault against its bond.
- **Ties never pass** unless the bar is at most one half.
- **Below the bar is an answer too.** The contract says what happens: a wider jury, a refund, or a human.

The bar is what lets a reader trust a ruling without trusting any one model: several model families had to reach the same answer independently.

**Status.** Built (CORE). The Solana program counts agreement the same way, in parts per million. See the [specification](/spec).
