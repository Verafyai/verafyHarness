# Policies and Covenant Launch

*Rules a wallet can't break, intent a jury reads, and the questions only a jury can answer.*

**Rules** are checks any computer runs on the decoded transaction: which programs it may call, how much may leave per transaction and per window, to whom, and on what release schedule. A violation is final.

**Intent and judgment** are for the jury: what the wallet is for, and questions such as "does this look like a dump on holders?".

**Covenant Launch.** The creator's share of a new token sits in one visible wallet that may only release it on a published schedule, to published addresses. Buyers read the covenant before they buy. The signer enforces it on every transaction.

**Always refused:** unreadable transactions, handing over token accounts, removing the signer, lowering the threshold so the humans can pass alone, shortening the time lock, and Squads spending limits.

**Status.** Built in core (SIGN-002): rules, the three templates (covenant launch, treasury, upgrade authority), tests. Design: [VIP-0011](https://github.com/Verafyai/verafyHarness/blob/main/docs/proposals/VIP-0011-policies.md).
